# Gnomes: the hidden neighbours

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A second people in the valley. Gnomes live in burrows under meadow edges, come out at dusk, farm mushrooms, copy a camp's workshop, borrow made things and repay them, and move away when a village grows loud. They never attack.

**Architecture:** A species row, a life clock, and a `chooseTask` branch, like the sprites. Burrows are caves of kind `burrow` with owner `gnome`, dug by the same carving helpers as fox burrows, with a mushroom patch (a new feature that regrows in `growPlants`). Three gnome actions join `START` (`shrooms`, `huddle`, `borrow`, `repay`); one per-500-tick rule, `gnomeTick`, handles copying, leaving, and disturbance. A goal card "The hidden neighbours" appears after first contact, like the sprites' card.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JavaScript scripts sharing one scope under `src/sim/`. Node's built-in test runner.

## Global Constraints

- Spec: `design/specs/2026-09-17-crafts-and-neighbours-design.md` section 4, all rows.
- Rules read data tables. Files in `src/sim/` are plain scripts, no `import` or `export`. Every new behaviour is visible: chronicle line, thought, card row, goal text, legend entry.
- Gnomes never attack. They flee people with brands and wolves. They ignore sprites and sprites ignore them.
- Every task that changes behaviour re-blesses the golden record after reading the counts; the soak stays green (human deaths only old age or one den death; nobody cut off; each seed under 25 seconds).
- Commit after every task on branch `dev`, by path, with your attribution trailer. Match on text, not line numbers.
