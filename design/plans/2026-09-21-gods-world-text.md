# The words for "country": proposed text

The player never sees a country, so the word goes. The creation text of recommendation 3 already calls a region "land", "a piece of land", or "a stretch of land". This text uses "land" for the same thing. The lines the gods drew are "borders", which the creation text also uses ("marks a border across the land").

`{g}` is a god's name, `{e}` its epithet, and `{n}` a number word from `nOf` ("three"). `{poles}` is a list such as "wet and still". `{why}` is the reason on the newest mark, for example "Kesh made it wet".

| # | Where the player reads it | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A god's thought, when another god marks over its land | `{g} marked over my country` | `{g} marked over my land` |
| 2 | The reply when an outside act names no act | `An option is an act and the country it falls on.` | `An option is an act and the land it falls on.` |
| 3 | The reply to the C key in the ages | `The field is all there is. The countries show after the valley is made.` | `The borders show on the valley once it is made.` |
| 4 | The phrase for a region, in cards and the sector summary | `a country that is {poles}. {why}` | `land that is {poles}. {why}` |
| 5 | The same phrase, for a region that no longer exists | `no country` | `no land` |
| 6 | The row label for that phrase, on the region card and the tile card | `Country` | `Land` |
| 7 | A god's card, where it stands | `It stands in a country that is {poles}. {why}.` | `It stands on land that is {poles}. {why}.` |
| 8 | The status line in the ages | `{n} countries, {n} gods awake` | `{n} pieces of land, {n} gods awake` |
| 9 | The caption while a god weighs its choice | `{g} weighs {n} countries.` | `{g} looks at {n} pieces of land.` |
| 10 | The button in the day era | `Countries` | `Borders` |
| 11 | The button's tooltip | `Show or hide the countries the gods made` | `Show or hide the borders the gods drew` |
| 12 | The key list entry for C | `Show or hide the countries` | `Show or hide the borders the gods drew` |

Row 6 and row 4 appear together: "Land: land that is wet and still. Kesh made it wet." The panel should say whether the repeat reads badly.
