# The words for "country": approved text

The player never sees a country, so the word goes. The creation text of recommendation 3 already calls a region "land", "a piece of land", or "a stretch of land". This text uses "land" for the same thing. The lines the gods drew are "borders", which the creation text also uses ("marks a border across the land").

`{g}` is a god's name, `{e}` its epithet, and `{n}` a number from `nOf` ("3"). `{poles}` is a list such as "wet and still". `{why}` is the reason on the newest mark, for example "Kesh made it wet".

A panel of three Sonnet readers reviewed this table on 21 Sept 2026. The table above holds their changes.

- Rows 3 and 9: all three readers wanted row 3 changed, and two gave the text above. Two of three kept "weighs" in row 9.
- Row 6: all three found "Land: land that is wet and still" clumsy. The card row now leaves the noun to the label. Row 4 keeps "land that is" where it stands without a label: the act card, the god card, and the sector summary.
- Row 8: two of three kept it.
- Row 12: two of three wanted the key list entry short, as the other entries are.
