# Closing: the cave goals, den contention, camp-site scoring, and the god interface

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give people a reason to go into the dark and a way to take a den: search a cave for its find with a brand, clear fallen rock with the axe, clear a den with a party, and lose it again when the fire fails. Score camp sites on stone and dens. Rename the player's fire to lightning and make it strike like the sky's, and tidy the other tools.

**Architecture:** Three hand-written goals in `src/sim/goals.js` (`caves`, `dens`, and their offers), three task starters in `src/sim/tasks.js` that take a brand from the pit the way the wolf guard does, a den record's `cleared` field with a reversion rule in `updateCamps`, two lines in `chooseSite`, and the interface changes in `src/ui.js`, `src/sim/main.js`, and the page template.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JavaScript scripts sharing one scope under `src/sim/`. Node's built-in test runner. Playwright for the page check.

## Global Constraints

- Specs: `design/specs/2026-09-17-topography-design.md` sections 4 (Contention), 5, 6 (God interface); `design/specs/2026-09-17-crafts-and-neighbours-design.md` section 5.
- Rules read data tables. Files in `src/sim/` are plain scripts, no `import` or `export`. Every new behaviour is visible.
- Deaths in the dark on a search are deaths that are not old age; the soak allows none. A search must end well or end with the person walking out: the ember's life and the dark rule are the danger, not death. If the soak shows a death on a search, the rule is wrong, not the test.
- Every task that changes behaviour re-blesses the golden record after reading the counts; the soak stays green; each seed under 25 seconds.
- Commit after every task on branch `dev`, by path, with your attribution trailer. Match on text, not line numbers.
