# The kinds each old-tongue meaning suits

The phrases in this file are the approved wording in `approved-text.md`. Five rows were corrected on
19 September after the panel changed them: "the dry room" to "the dry hole", "the long hollow" to
"the low crawl", "the leaning trees" to "where the branches tangle", "the slow water" to "the slow
current", and "the high back" to "the high ridge". Task 1 uses this file.

Five kinds take an old name: `water`, `hill`, `cave`, `grove`, `ford`. A phrase carries the set of
kinds it suits. A phrase that says nothing about the shape of the place carries all five.

Counts below are the supply per kind, including the ten new phrases. The heaviest measured demand
at day 1 across six seeds is: hills 18, caves 10, ponds 9, groves 8, fords 1, water 1. Every kind
has slack. The hill figure is seed gamma, measured by the task 1 reviewer and confirmed unchanged
at day 70.
