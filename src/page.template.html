Things to try

  • Wait for the fire pit, then use Light fire on it. Then strike the grass instead and compare.
  • Let the fire go out. Watch the goals, the moods, and who comes to fix it.
  • Pin a rabbit and watch its decisions when a fox or a person comes close.
  • Turn Stock food off and see what the newcomers do when they arrive.
  • Nudge someone who is sitting by the fire when the woodpile is empty.
  • Open the world map and point at sectors. People go where the resources are. M cycles the sector, the nearby view, and the world map.
  • Keep the hearth lit three days straight. Watch the goal list open up: axe, logs, lean-to, waterskin, spear.
  • Let the fire go out at night with meat in the stash. Wolves notice.
  • Watch a winter. Berries stop, deer thin out, and only a camp with a rack and smoked meat is comfortable.
  • Wait for someone to see a light at the edge of the firelight. Then set an offering stone, or a ward, or neither, and watch what the difference costs.
  • Cut trees in a grove sector on purpose. Count the nights until the fire goes out on its own.
  • Pin an elder and watch who sits with them. Skills pass by the fire.
  • When a camp reaches five people with a roof, two of them will leave in spring to found another. Switch camps with the tabs in the strip.
