The release workflow copies a section into the notes of its GitHub Release. Write each section so that a reader needs no other section.

## v0.1.0 (2026-09-20)

This is the first numbered release. Until today the only public page was the dev site, which changes at every merge. The released page at https://earchibald.github.io/hamlet/ changes only at a release.

### What you get
- Hearth is one HTML file. It needs no install and no account.
- Every world starts with its creation. The primal gods split the land into countries and mark them, age by age. The valley you play in is built from those marks.
- You play a god. You do not give orders. You set goals. You give the people fire. You move the camp site before it is built. You poke people. The people decide the rest for themselves.
- The valley has a river, hills, caves, and five levels. Wolves, foxes, deer, rabbits, gnomes, and sprites live in it.
- The chronicle records what happens, in plain sentences.

### What to know
- The engine now counts time in real units. A world day is 86,400 ticks. A long stretch of days takes longer to run than it did before.
- The work that makes long runs fast again is not finished. Six of the long test files are switched off until that work is done. The fast suite for this build ran 395 tests: 384 passed, none failed, and 11 were skipped.
- We will finish that work before the next release.
